# Product

## Platform
web

## Stack
React and TypeScript, consistent with the user's portfolio project.

## Users
Prospective dental patients exploring a fictional clinic; the site also demonstrates the developer's portfolio capabilities.

## Product Purpose
Present the fictional Lúmina Odontologia, its care and space, and help visitors find a way to make contact.

## Capabilities and Constraints
The clinic is entirely fictional. Do not invent real credentials, testimonials, contact details, or appointments. Use the five images supplied by the user. Build in the dedicated private repository.

## Brand Commitments
Lúmina Odontologia. The user chose dentistegagnon.com as a visual, text composition and motion reference. The images were supplied by the user.

## Product Principles
Communicate warmth and clarity; support keyboard and reduced-motion users; make the fictional nature and simulated interactions clear.
