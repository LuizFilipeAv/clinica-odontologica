# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Vite + React 19 + TypeScript (strict), CSS Modules, GSAP + ScrollTrigger and Lenis.

## Users
Prospective dental patients exploring a fictional clinic; the site also demonstrates the developer's portfolio capabilities.

## Product Purpose
Present the fictional Alva Odontologia, its care and space, and help visitors find a way to make contact (simulated booking).

## Capabilities and Constraints
The clinic is entirely fictional. Do not invent real credentials, testimonials, contact details, or appointments; the booking widget is simulated. Use the images supplied by the user; new ones may be generated from the prompts in docs/briefing.md. Build in the dedicated private repository.

## Brand Commitments
Alva Odontologia. The palette (teal, cream, coral) was chosen by the user. The logo reference supplied by the user was renamed (original name: Alva), and the visual base is the user's petshop project, translated to dentistry. The images were supplied by the user.

## Product Principles
Communicate warmth and clarity ("precisão acolhedora"); support keyboard and reduced-motion users; make the fictional nature and simulated interactions clear.
